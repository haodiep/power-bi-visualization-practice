\# MiniViz May Week 3 – Power BI Visualization Practice



\## Overview



This project is part of my Power BI visualization practice. The dashboard was recreated from the \*\*MiniViz May Week 3\*\* challenge with the theme:



> \*\*What Dominates Your Life?\*\*



The dataset is fictional and was created for learning and exploration. The main goal of this practice is to improve my ability to design a clear treemap visualization and explain what the chart shows through simple visual storytelling.

\## Objective



The objective of this dashboard is to show which life areas and activities take up the largest share of time.



The treemap helps viewers quickly identify the most dominant categories based on their relative size.



\## Key Visual Takeaways



\* The largest blocks in the treemap represent the life areas or activities that take up the highest share of total time.

\* Smaller blocks represent activities with a lower contribution to the overall total.

\* The treemap is useful for showing proportional differences because viewers can compare categories by area size.

\* Breaking the data into life areas, sub-activities, and activities helps create a more detailed view of how time is distributed.

\* The visual answers one clear question: what dominates the overall time distribution?



\## Design Notes



In this dashboard, I focused on:



\* Using a treemap because the main question is about proportion and dominance

\* Keeping the layout simple so the visual remains the main focus

\* Using clear category grouping to make the hierarchy easier to understand

\* Adding a short title to communicate the purpose of the dashboard

\* Reducing visual clutter to support quick interpretation
