# Power BI Visualization Practice

This repository documents my Power BI visualization practice projects, with a focus on dashboard design, visual storytelling, insight writing, and business reporting skills.

The purpose of this repository is not only to store Power BI files, but also to practice explaining what each visualization shows, why it matters, and how the dashboard can support better business understanding.

## Purpose

As an aspiring Business Analyst / Data Analyst / Reporting Analyst, I use this repository to improve my ability to:

* Build clear and well-structured Power BI dashboards
* Choose appropriate visualizations for different business questions
* Design dashboards with better layout, spacing, and visual hierarchy
* Write concise insights based on charts and patterns
* Practice data storytelling from a business perspective
* Document my learning process and dashboard design decisions

## Storytelling Framework

For each dashboard, I try to practice the following storytelling structure:

```text
Observation → Meaning → Business implication
```

This means I do not only describe what is visible in the chart. I also try to explain what the pattern may suggest and why it could matter from a business perspective.

Example:

```text
Observation:
One category shows significantly higher performance than the others.

Meaning:
This suggests that performance is not evenly distributed across all categories.

Business implication:
The business may want to investigate what is driving the stronger performance and whether similar strategies can be applied to lower-performing categories.
```

Instead of only creating charts, I want to develop the habit of asking:

* What should the viewer notice first?
